# Product retest — 14 October 2026

## Latest: material content review

The creator authorized relaxation of overly strict review. Reviewer complaints now receive a final evidence-based decision before blocking a story. Harmless tense, synonyms, camera differences, supporting characters, and absence after a sacrifice are allowed. Missing requested steps, wrong actors, forbidden outcomes, and reversed action order remain blocking. Invalid reviewer evidence causes a retry or error, not automatic approval. Cleared complaints are retained as advisories and removed from required rewrite feedback.

- Fresh generation and app review: **8/8 passed** across Telugu, Hindi, Tamil and Marathi (`script_fidelity_benchmark_2026-10-14_material_review.json`). Editorial scores remained **84–88/100**; scores were not forced to 90.
- Authored review controls: **20/20 correct**, accepting ten valid examples and rejecting ten deliberately broken examples (`material_review_calibration.json`). These controls test reviewer classification, not the entire generation pipeline.
- This is a **100% automated pass rate on this small, previously used benchmark**. It does not establish 90% population accuracy or precision, and is not an independent held-out evaluation. Native-speaker manual review remains the next validation step.
- The latest changes concern script approval; they do not provide new evidence about image generation, voices, or video exports beyond the earlier tests recorded below.

## Changes
