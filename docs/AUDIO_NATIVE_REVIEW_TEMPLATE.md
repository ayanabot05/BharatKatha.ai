# Fluent-speaker audio review form

Use one form for each language, model revision and three-case run. Keep the reviewer's identity private; record their language fluency and region without publishing contact details. Do not fill a score on the model's behalf.

Review the three files blind to automated quality claims when possible. A PASS means the reviewer explicitly accepted the audio for the stated content and variety; it does not mean 100% linguistic accuracy. Record dissenting reviews and recheck regenerated files. Caption synchronization and MP4 playback require separate application evidence.
